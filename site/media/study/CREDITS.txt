2026-11-22 selected study layout

The room, furniture, desktop props, glazing and near foliage use native
Three.js geometry. The monitor previews show the project page.

ming-courtyard-far-day.webp and ming-courtyard-far-night.webp derive from the
glass area in the user-supplied reference/user-layout.png. The daylight image
was edited with OpenAI image generation to remove the foreground desk lamp.
The resulting garden was relit with the same image-generation tool for the
night image. Both images were framed and encoded locally to 880 × 652 WebP and
appear on the distant garden surface in the 3D scene. study-mountain-art.webp
is the small framed artwork area from the supplied reference. The public
redistribution license for that supplied image and its derivatives is unspecified.

The plaster albedo is derived from the credited Poly Haven clay plaster.
tools/weave-rug.py generates the repeating jute material.
The RGB lighting textures record indirect occlusion, window sun and desk-lamp
visibility, respectively. The four sun-wind maps store canopy transmission
at three bend angles, sampled against the same fixed window and furniture.
These lighting maps and both room reflections are generated from this layout
by tools/bake-study.py.
